WHO WE ARE

Growing from
deep roots.

Established in 1984, ELADIDI INTEGRATED FARMS SERVICE LTD is a trusted agricultural enterprise rooted in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. For over forty years we have turned fertile land into reliable produce, dependable supply and meaningful opportunity for processors, retailers and communities across the region.

Our principal activities centre on rubber plantation development, pure natural latex production and quality pineapple cultivation. We tap latex directly from our own rubber trees and move it carefully for processing — without additives — so customers receive produce they can trust. We also harvest and supply naturally ripened pineapples to processing factories, retailers and customers across Edo State and beyond.

With a reported agricultural landholding of approximately 1,000 hectares, we continue to expand productive capacity, deepen relationships across the produce and processing value chain, and invest in the people and communities that surround our farms.
